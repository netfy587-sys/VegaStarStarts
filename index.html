Chapter 1Why breakfast matters

Breakfast is the first small decision of the day. In many homes it is also the calmest meal, before the rush begins. Across the world, people start mornings with rice, bread, eggs, fruit, tea and coffee, each with its own story.

Not everything powerful has to look complicated. A good breakfast can be a bowl of oats, a slice of toast or leftover rice with an egg. Small changes create big impacts: adding one fruit or one glass of water to your morning can lift your energy.

Anytime, Anywhere, Anything: breakfast can be eaten at a table, on a balcony, on a train or even as an evening treat.

Chapter 4Around the world

Tap a place to see a classic morning meal.

India

Poha, upma, idli-sambar, parathas with curd and masala chai are all loved. Recipes vary widely by region, from soft steamed idlis in the south to stuffed parathas in the north.

Japan

A traditional breakfast often includes steamed rice, miso soup, grilled fish, pickles and egg. It is light, balanced and served in small portions.

Mexico

Chilaquiles (tortilla pieces in salsa), huevos rancheros and fresh fruit are popular. Warm tortillas and beans often join the plate.

England

The "full English" brings eggs, toast, beans, tomatoes and more. Many people also enjoy simple tea with toast or porridge on ordinary days.

Chapter 6Your first-morning plan

Try it out today and you'll never be disappointed. Here is a simple routine.

  1. Plan the night before. Decide the menu and lay out what you need.
  2. Start with water. A glass of water wakes up the body gently.
  3. Pick a protein. Eggs, curd, lentils, nuts or paneer keep you full longer.
  4. Add something fresh. Fruit, cucumber or tomato for colour and vitamins.
  5. Sit and enjoy. Eat slowly, without screens, even for just ten minutes.

Chapter 9Quick answers

What if I am not hungry in the morning?

Start with something small, like fruit or a smoothie, and see how your body responds.

Can I prepare breakfast the night before?

Yes. Overnight oats, chopped fruit and boiled eggs all work well.

Is skipping breakfast bad?

It depends on the person. Some people feel fine without it, while others need it. Listen to your body and ask a doctor if unsure.

How do I make breakfast fun for kids?

Use colours, shapes and simple choices so they can help build their own plates.
